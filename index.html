Tech Career Pathfinder

📱 Account & Data Removal

We respect your privacy rights. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from Tech Career Pathfinder, you can request deletion by following the instructions below.

📧 Option 1: Request by Email

Send an email from the address associated with your account to:

✉️ privacy@fajostech.com

Please include your account email in the request. We will process your request within 30 days.

🛜 Option 2: In-App Deletion

You can also delete your account directly from within the app:

  • 1 Open Tech Career Pathfinder on your device
  • 2 Go to Settings from the bottom navigation bar
  • 3 Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"

⚠️ In-app deletion is available only if you have the app installed and are logged in.

📋 What data will be deleted?
We will permanently delete your profile information, saved careers, quiz history, learning progress, and any other personal data associated with your account.

⚠️ Important Note
Once your account is deleted, this action is irreversible and cannot be undone. Please ensure you have exported any important data before proceeding.
